## Mentoring Committee for Higher Studies

The mentoring committee for higher studies guides VIT students for Postgraduate, Doctoral and Post Doctoral studies through live mentoring sessions with Internationally renowned academics and Alumni. It helps with structuring of Statement of Purpose Essays and Publications, Interview guidance, and research papers essential for securing scholarships and funded admissions.
