### What is VITREE?

VITREE stands for VIT Research Entrance Examination. It is the mandatory Computer-Based Test for admission to Ph.D., Direct Ph.D. and Ph.D. in Deep Technology programmes at VIT.

### How many sessions does VITREE run per year?

VITREE is conducted for two sessions per year: July and January. This page covers the July 2026 session. January 2026 session results have already been declared.

### What is the difference between Ph.D. and Direct Ph.D. at VIT?

The Ph.D. requires a postgraduate (Master’s) degree for entry. The Direct Ph.D. allows B.E./B.Tech. graduates with First Class to bypass a separate Master’s degree and proceed directly to doctoral research, on a full-time basis only.

### What is the Ph.D. in Deep Technology at VIT?

The Ph.D. in Deep Technology is a Chancellor Fellowship doctoral programme for research in nationally strategic deep-tech domains. It requires a separate application template and is only available as Internal Full Time.

### What is the VITREE exam pattern?

VITREE is a 2-hour Computer-Based Test with 100 MCQs. For Ph.D.: 70 Technical, 15 English Communication, 15 Statistics and Probability. For Direct Ph.D.: 80 Technical and 20 English Communication.

### What is the eligibility for the Direct Ph.D. at VIT?

A B.E. or B.Tech. degree with First Class from a recognised university. Candidates do not need a Master’s degree but must apply for Internal Full Time only.

### Is a research proposal required for VITREE?

Yes. All candidates must submit a two-page research proposal at the time of application, using the prescribed template: one template for Ph.D. and Direct Ph.D., and a separate template for the Deep Technology Chancellor Fellowship programme.

### How is the Ph.D. merit list prepared?

Selection combines the VITREE CBT score, marks in the qualifying degree (PG for Ph.D., UG for Direct Ph.D.), the research proposal, and performance in the Personal Interview.

### Can the interview date or programme be changed after applying?

No. Change of test venue, campus, date of interview or programme is not permitted after submission.

### What is the tuition fee for a Ph.D. at VIT?

Ph.D. Internal Full Time: Rs. 40,000 per annum. Ph.D. External Part Time (Teachers): Rs. 60,000 per annum. Ph.D. External Part Time (Industry): Rs. 75,000 per annum. Direct Ph.D. and Ph.D. Deep Technology: Rs. 40,000 per annum each. A refundable Caution Deposit of Rs. 3,000 and a one-time Thesis Fee of Rs. 10,000 (paid at thesis submission) also apply.

### When should the Thesis Fee be paid?

The Thesis Fee of Rs. 10,000 is paid at the time of thesis submission only, not at the time of admission.

### Where can I find the list of available research guides at VIT?

The Research Guide Directory for the July 2026 session is available at vit.ac.in/research-guide-directory-2026, listing guides by school and research area.

### Can foreign nationals or NRI candidates apply for PhD at VIT?

Yes, through a separate NRI/Foreign Research Admissions application at admissions.vit.ac.in/researchirapplication/login, with a different fee structure.

The VITREE Syllabus, Eligibility Criteria document and Information Brochure are linked from the research admissions page at vit.ac.in/admissions/research.
