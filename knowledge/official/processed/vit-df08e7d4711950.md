## Experience Global Education with VIT's International Transfer Programme (ITP)

VIT’s International Transfer Programme (ITP) offers students an exceptional opportunity to pursue a global education by completing part of their degree program at VIT and the remining credits at a prestigious partner university abroad. Depending on the program structure, students can earn a degree from VIT, the partner university, or both, giving them a competitive edge in the global job market.

## Our International Transfer Programmes Include:

**2+2 Bachelor Program:**

* + Spend the first two years at VIT and the next two years at a partner university.
 + Graduate with a globally recognized Bachelor’s degree.

**1+1 Master Program:**

* + First year of your Master program at VIT and the second year at our partner university.
 + Earn a Master’s degree enriched by international experience.

**3.5+1.5 Accelerated Pathway Program:**

* + Earn your Bachelor’s degree from VIT and pursue a Master’s degree from partner university.
 + Seamless transition to advanced studies at a partner institution.

**3+2 Program:**

* + Spend three years at VIT and two years at a partner university.
 + Graduate with a combination of degrees that opens doors to global opportunities.

**1+3 Semesters Pathway Program**

* + Join Master’s Program in VIT and earn the Degree from partner University

These programs are crafted in collaboration with esteemed universities across the globe, offering a diverse and enriching academic journey.

Embark on a path to global success with VIT’s International Transfer Programme.
