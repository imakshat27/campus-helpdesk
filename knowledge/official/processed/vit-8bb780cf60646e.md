VIT is dedicated to fostering sustainability on all fronts, including social, economic, and environmental. As current research, trends, and cultural attitudes continue to grow, so does our understanding of sustainability. Given that the UN SDGs address some of the most important issues of our day, we acknowledge their significance in directing our efforts.

Good health and well-being, lowering air pollution, clean water, reducing and diverting waste, collaborating with the campus community, conserving energy and water, and reducing carbon emissions are some of the current projects of VIT Sustainability. By integrating sustainability into research, teaching and learning, and outreach efforts, VIT supports the SDGs and makes a substantial contribution to the creation of a more sustainable future for all. We have set an ambitious goal to achieve net zero by 2050, and we will keep making investments that bring us closer to that objective.
