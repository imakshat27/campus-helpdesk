## Ranking and Recognition

Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), Vellore was established in 1984 as Vellore Engineering College, with 180 students and 3 programs. Today it has evolved in to a deemed to be University with over 40,000 students offering 46 Undergraduate and 48 Postgraduate programs and having 19 schools in two campuses.

The Directorate of Ranking and Accreditation (RAAC) at Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) is committed to enhancing the university’s reputation. We focus on implementing quality measures that improve VIT’s standing both nationally and globally. Through strategic initiatives and continuous evaluation, we strive to position VIT among the leading educational institutions.

VIT has grown as a leading Educational Institution in India. National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) has accredited VIT Vellore with CGPA of 3.66 on four-point scale at A++ grade. National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF), Government of India, has ranked VIT Vellore among the Top 20 Universities in the country consecutively since 2016. In NIRF 2025 VIT ranked 14 University category, at 14 under Research, 16 under Engineering and at 21 in the Overall category.

VIT has further strengthened its global academic standing in the QS World University Rankings by Subject 2026, with several disciplines achieving impressive international rankings. In the broad subject area of Engineering & Technology, VIT is ranked 119th globally, reaffirming its position among the world’s leading engineering institutions.

The University has demonstrated exceptional performance across key engineering and technology disciplines, ranking 86th globally in Computer Science & Information Systems, 87th in Electrical & Electronic Engineering, 137th in Materials Science, 148th in Mechanical, Aeronautical & Manufacturing Engineering, and within the 101–200 band for Data Science & Artificial Intelligence. It is also ranked in the 201–250 band for Chemical Engineering and 201–275 for Civil & Structural Engineering.

Beyond engineering, VIT has earned strong global recognition across the natural sciences. The University is ranked 303rd in the broad subject area of Natural Sciences, with notable subject rankings of 104th in Mathematics, 251–300 in Chemistry and Physics & Astronomy, and 351–400 in Environmental Sciences.

In the Life Sciences & Medicine broad subject area, VIT is placed in the 501–550 band globally. Its subject-specific achievements include rankings of 301–350 in Biological Sciences, 401–475 in Agriculture & Forestry, and 551–600 in Medicine.
VIT has also established a growing presence in the social sciences and creative disciplines. The University is ranked in the 451–500 band for Business & Management Studies, 251–275 for Statistics & Operational Research, and 201–300 for Arts & Design.

These rankings underscore VIT’s sustained commitment to academic excellence, impactful research, and interdisciplinary innovation across a diverse range of subject areas, reinforcing its reputation as a globally recognized institution of higher education. VIT is ranked 691st globally in the QS World University Rankings 2027. VIT achieved a significant milestone in the Shanghai Ranking (ARWU) 2025 by securing in the 501–600 global rank band and attaining the 1–2 rank band among Indian institutions. It shows our excellence in research and global academic standing.
As a testament to its unwavering commitment to sustainability and environmentally responsible practices, Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) has secured a distinguished position in the QS Sustainability Rankings 2026, securing 352nd place globally and 7th among higher educational institutions in India. This achievement reflects VIT’s continued dedication to advancing environmental stewardship, social impact, and effective governance while contributing to a more sustainable future.

In research excellence, 90 faculty members of Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) have been featured among the World’s Top 2% Scientists in the 2025 by Stanford University, USA.

Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) is committed to advancing society through excellence in education, research, innovation, and community engagement. Its strong pursuit of academic excellence and global standards continues to make VIT a preferred destination for students from India and across the world.

## Directorate of Ranking and Accreditation

The Directorate of Ranking and Accreditation is dedicated to initiate strategic measures that foster academic excellence, research innovation, and institutional development by focusing on key performance indicators and collaborative efforts. We strive to position VIT among the premier institutions worldwide.

VISION

To excel in institutional performance through strategic initiatives and continuous improvement.

### MISSION

**Promote Excellence:** Foster a culture of excellence by implementing best practices in teaching, research, academic administration and community engagement.
**Data-Driven Approach:** Collect and analyse data that supports informed decision-making and enhances the institutional performance in national and international rankings.
**Continuous Improvement:**Identify areas for improvement and promote initiatives that enhance the overall educational experience and institutional effectiveness.

## Key Functions

* **Data Collection:** Gathering institutional data required for various Rankings and Institution of Eminence Project.
* **Data Analytics:** Analysing performance metrics to assess institutional standing and identify areas for improvement.
* **Data Submission:** Compiling and submitting data to national and international ranking and other agencies such as NIRF, AISHE, Quacquarelli Symonds (QS), Times Higher Education (THE), Shanghai Rankings, and US News.
* **Institutional Information Services:** : Serving as a central hub for ranking-related information, providing insights and reports to support strategic decision-making.

* **Enhance Institutional Visibility:** Improve the institution's national and global reputation by participating in prestigious ranking frameworks.
* **Integrity in Data Management:** Ensure the systematic collection, validation, and updation of institutional data.
* **Performance Analysis:** Conduct analysis of ranking metrics to identify strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Strategic Benchmarking:** Compare institutional performance with peer institutions.
* **Support Institutional Development:** Provide data-driven insights to attain the academic and research excellence.
* **Continuous Improvement:** Develop strategies to enhance institutional performance in various ranking parameters over time.

### Future Plans

* **Automated Data Capture:** Implement systems to automatically capture data on all events and activities directly at their source.
* **Predictive Analytics Models:** Create models based on various ranking frameworks to enable predictive analysis.
* **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Identify key performance indicators that will enhance our rankings.
* **Sustainability Initiatives:** Recommend policies to strengthen VIT's sustainability efforts, aligning with global environmental standards.
* **Stakeholder Awareness:** Raise awareness among stakeholders about different ranking frameworks and their significance.
* **Collaborative Workshops:** Organise workshops and training sessions to engage faculty, staff, and students in discussions on ranking strategies and best practices.
* **Benchmarking:** Conduct benchmarking studies against leading global institutions to find areas of improvement and adopt best practices.
* **Feedback Mechanisms:** Implement feedback mechanisms to collect insights from stakeholders, ensuring our strategies effectively enhance the institution's standings.
