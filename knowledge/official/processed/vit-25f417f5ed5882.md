## Placement Process

The online Tests will be conducted in the respective campuses monitored by the placement coordinators.

If you want the interview process to be done remotely, the Vellore campus placement team shall coordinate the same as done in the last 3 years.

Physical interviews for the shortlisted students are conducted in any of the VIT Campuses based on the convenience of the recruiters and the students from other VIT campuses are virtually connected.
