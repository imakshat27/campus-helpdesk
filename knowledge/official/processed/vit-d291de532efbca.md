## MBA Placement Records

Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) University offers a two-year Master of Business Administration (MBA) program designed to provide students with the skills and knowledge required to become successful business leaders. Here is some information about the MBA program offered by VIT University:

* **Curriculum:** The MBA program at VIT University covers various management subjects, including accounting, economics, marketing, operations, and human resources. The curriculum is designed to develop students’ analytical, critical thinking, and decision-making skills through a combination of classroom lectures, case studies, group discussions, and projects.
* **Faculty:** The MBA program at VIT University has a team of experienced faculty members who have extensive industry and academic experience. They use their expertise to guide students and provide them with insights into real-world business scenarios.
* **Industry Exposure:** VIT University’s MBA program provides students with ample opportunities to interact with industry professionals through guest lectures, industry visits, and internships. This exposure helps students to understand the current business trends, challenges, and opportunities.

Which programmes are covered by VIT placements?

B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, MCA, M.Sc, Life Sciences programmes (SBST), Integrated programmes, and three-year UG programmes across Science, Commerce and Humanities.

What sectors do VIT recruiters come from?

IT products and software, software services, consulting, data analytics, automobile and core engineering, BFSI, pharmaceutical and biotech, and management-specialist sectors for MBA graduates.

Which companies recruit from VIT?

Recruiters include Microsoft, Amazon, Oracle, Societe Generale and more than 850 other companies spanning technology, consulting, BFSI, manufacturing and life sciences. The full recruiter list is updated each year by CDC.

Does VIT have a dedicated placement cell?

Yes. The Career Development Centre (CDC) is a dedicated, university-wide unit that coordinates placement and internship operations across all four VIT campuses centrally.

Who heads the VIT Career Development Centre?

The CDC is headed by Dr. V. Samuel Rajkumar (BE, MBA, Ph.D.), Director, CDC, VIT Vellore.

Does VIT provide internship placement support as well?

Yes. The CDC facilitates both summer and winter internships alongside final placements, and several internship programmes include a pre-placement offer pathway.

What is a Super Dream Offer at VIT?

VIT’s CDC classifies offers by CTC threshold into categories; Super Dream Offers are those above the higher published CTC threshold in a given cycle, representing the highest salary tier among all offers recorded. The specific CTC cut-off defining Super Dream is published by CDC each year.

What is a Dream Offer at VIT?

Dream Offers are placement offers above a published CTC threshold below the Super Dream tier. Both Super Dream and Dream classifications are defined by CDC for each placement cycle.

Does VIT guarantee placements?

VIT does not publish or guarantee a fixed placement rate. Placement statistics represent actual recorded outcomes in a given cycle; individual outcomes depend on academic performance, programme, recruiter participation in that year’s cycle, and individual performance in recruitment processes.

How does VIT prepare students for placements?

Career Management courses are integrated into the B.Tech and MBA curricula, covering communication, aptitude, group discussions and interview preparation. The CDC runs mock interviews and company-specific preparation sessions before recruitment drives.

Where can I find the full VIT placement recruiter list?

The current recruiter list is maintained by the Career Development Centre. Contact CDC at placement@vit.ac.in or visit vit.ac.in/cdc-highlights for the latest published list.
