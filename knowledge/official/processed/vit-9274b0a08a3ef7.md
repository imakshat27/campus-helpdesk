## Who is eligible to apply under FOREIGN category?

* Any foreigner with a valid passport (passport is not mandatory for Nepal and Bhutan candidates).
* OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) / PIO (Persons of Indian Origin) Candidates.

**Note:** Aadhaar Card is not proof of citizenship.

## Who is eligible to apply under NRI category?

* Indian students whose parents are currently working abroad with a valid work VISA.

**Note:** Aadhaar Card is not proof of citizenship.

## List of documents to be submitted at the time of reporting for students admitted in NRI / Foreign category.

* Transfer / Migration / College Leaving Certificate obtained from the Institute last studied (as applicable)
* Student Profile
* Recent passport size color photos - 2 Nos

* Physical Fitness Certificate (Certified by authorized Physician) - Download
* Common Affidavit – (Common Affidavit for Student, Parent/Guardian & Hostel )
* Undertaking form - (Signed by Student & Parent) - Download
* **Parents Employer Certificate - (only for NRI candidates)** - Download
* **Sponsorship letter from parents - (only for NRI candidates)** - Download

## Copy of Documents (1 set)

* Admission letter
* 10th grade / O level and 12th grade / A level / Form VI.
* UG Consolidated / Semester wise mark sheet until final year.
* Qualifying UG Degree or Provisional Certificate.
* For NRI candidates, copy of Passport & VISA of their parents.
* For Foreign candidates, copy of Passport & VISA of the applicant.
