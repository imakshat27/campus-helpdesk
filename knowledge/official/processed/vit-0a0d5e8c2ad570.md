### Which VIT PG programmes require VITMEE 2026?

M.Tech, MCA, M.Des, and M.Sc (AI&ML, Data Science) require VITMEE 2026. M.Tech also accepts a valid GATE score. MBA admission uses VITBEE, CAT, XAT, or MAT. Other PG programmes admit students through direct application.

### What is the last date to apply for VITMEE 2026?

The last date to apply for VITMEE 2026 (M.Tech, MCA, and M.Des programmes) is 12 April 2026. Specific dates for other PG programmes are published on the respective VIT application portals.

### Which VIT PG programmes admit students without an entrance test?

M.Sc (2-year, non-VITMEE streams), MSW, M.Arch, LL.M, and the 5-year Integrated M.Tech and M.Sc programmes admit students directly through application, with selection based on academic record.

### Are scholarships available for VIT PG students?

Yes. VIT offers merit-based scholarships linked to entrance examination performance and academic record. Government scholarships and Vidya Lakshmi education loans are also accepted.

### How do I choose the right VIT PG programme?

Match your undergraduate background and career direction to the programme. Engineering graduates can apply for M.Tech, MCA, or M.Des through VITMEE. Graduates from any discipline can apply for MBA, MSW, or relevant M.Sc programmes. Class 12 students aiming at a longer pathway can consider the 5-year Integrated M.Tech or M.Sc programmes.

### Which PG programmes are available for engineering graduates?

Engineering graduates can apply for M.Tech (VITMEE or GATE), MCA (VITMEE), M.Des (VITMEE), M.Sc in AI and Machine Learning or Data Science (VITMEE), MBA (VITBEE/CAT/XAT/MAT), and M.Arch (for architecture graduates).

### What is the duration of VIT PG programmes?

Most VIT PG programmes are 2 years (M.Tech, MBA, MCA, M.Des, M.Sc, MSW, M.Arch). The Integrated M.Tech and M.Sc programmes are 5 years. LL.M and Master’s Overseas Program durations are as per the published programme.

### Can students apply to multiple VIT PG programmes?

Yes. Students can apply to multiple PG programmes through the respective official application portals. Each programme has its own application form and application fee.
