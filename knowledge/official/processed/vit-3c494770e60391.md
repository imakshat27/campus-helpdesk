Message from the Director

Welcome to the International Relations Office of Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT). In today’s interconnected world, our mission is to internationalize the academic environment by enhancing international Student and faculty mobility and working to strengthen international research collaboration. Through strategic alliances, international programs, and cultural exchanges, we aim to build a community that transcends geographical boundaries.

Founded in 1984, the Vellore Institute of Technology(VIT) has established a rich tradition of excellence across various academic disciplines. VIT is one of India's top 10 institutions among higher education and research institutions and it has an international presence. VIT is committed to providing high-quality education without geographical boundaries, bringing diversity to its student body and research activities. VIT creates a unique holistic ecosystem for education that offers interactive learning, a very flexible academic structure, cutting-edge research, strong industry collaboration, and entrepreneurship. Our focus is to create opportunities for cross-cultural understanding, collaborative research, and innovative global solutions. Whether you are a prospective student, a partner organization, or an esteemed colleague, we are committed to supporting your journey toward impactful international engagement.

The International Relations Office has initiated several dynamic mobility programs aimed at fostering global collaboration and enriching academic experiences. These programs offer students, faculty, and researchers the opportunity to engage in exchange programs, internships, and joint research projects across partner institutions worldwide. VIT enjoys a relationship with universities worldwide and industries that go beyond academics and research collaborations. We are also interested in initiating bilateral partnerships with Universities and Research organisations of repute, which will benefit both students and the faculty community.

Together, we can address global challenges and build a more inclusive, sustainable future. I encourage you to explore the diverse opportunities we offer and connect with us to make a difference on the global stage.

With over 500+ international MoUs across the globe, Vellore Institute of Technology has a strong international presence abroad. The International Relations office of Vellore Institute of Technology helps the students to explore diverse environments and various cultures across the world through partnership arrangements with institutes and universities all over the world. International Relations team helps to connect students and faculty with peers from foreign institutions abroad. The international partner universities are spread in various countries throughout the world – USA, Canada, UK,Australia, Asia-Pacific, Europe, Middle East, Africa, and South America.

VIT provides education to students not only from India but also from abroad. The growing number of foreign students on campus to pursue their education and research stands as a testimony to VIT’s transformation into an International Institute.

The students get a chance to visit international partner universities in their **pre- final or final year** under the Semester Abroad Programme (SAP). VIT’s Semester Abroad programme aims to encourage students from all streams to pursue their courses and capstone project in one of the international partner universities. The Office of International Relations organizes a road show to students in their pre-final year and all are invited to participate. The Semester Abroad Programme is open to both undergraduate and postgraduate students. A Semester Abroad Programme is a transformative experience that equips students for a globalized world.

VIT’s International Transfer Programme (ITP) offers students an exceptional opportunity to pursue a global education by completing part of their degree at VIT and the remaining credits at a prestigious partner university abroad. Depending on the program structure, students can earn a degree from VIT, the partner university, or both, giving them a competitive edge in the global job market.The students can opt for the International Transfer Programme in partner universities in the USA, UK, Belgium, Sweden, New Zealand and Australia. This international program is offered to Engineering streams. The International Relations of VIT also brings in over 200+ faculty from foreign universities to take up teaching and research assignments at VIT. International programs don’t end here. There are internships ,summer and winter schools available all over the globe for the students to earn a fellowship, valuable work and study experience.

## Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) was signed between VIT and the prestigious Australian National University (ANU) in Canberra, Australia

A historic Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) was signed between VIT and the prestigious Australian National University (ANU) in Canberra, Australia. This agreement was signed by Dr. Sekar Viswanathan, the Vice President of VIT and Dr. Scott Pearsall marking a significant milestone in the university’s global academic and research collaborations.

Under the terms of this partnership, selected students from VIT will have the unique opportunity to pursue their academic programs in a hybrid format — partially in India at VIT and partially at ANU in Australia. Moreover, this MoU opens avenues for research collaborations between VIT and ANU, potentially leading to joint research projects, faculty exchanges, and further academic initiatives.
