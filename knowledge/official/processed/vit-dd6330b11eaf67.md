## School of Healthcare Science and Engineering (SHINE)

1. What makes this course unique compared to other contemporary courses?

This course stands out due to its unique interdisciplinary approach, effectively merging engineering, science, and medicine. It equips students with a comprehensive understanding of both healthcare principles and advanced technological tools, specifically preparing them for innovative roles in the rapidly evolving field of healthcare technology.

2. Why should I consider joining this course?

You should consider joining this course if you are interested in the dynamic intersection of healthcare and technology. It provides a deep understanding of how technology is fundamentally reshaping the future of healthcare, making it an excellent choice for those passionate about both the fields.

3. How is Health Sciences and Technology different from Biomedical Engineering and Biotechnology?

Health Sciences and Technology distinguishes itself by broadly integrating engineering principles directly with healthcare applications, with a significant focus on areas like AI and medical technology. This provides a wider scope compared to Biomedical Engineering, which typically concentrates more narrowly on medical devices, and Biotechnology, which primarily deals with biological systems.

4. What are the core subjects covered throughout the curriculum?

Core subjects provide a strong base in the interdisciplinary nature of the field. These include key areas such as Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare, Medical Electronics, Digital Signal Processing for Biomedical Applications and Applied Human Anatomy and Physiology. The curriculum also emphasizes practical training focused on the real-world application of technology in healthcare.

5. Is it suitable for students without a background in biology or mathematics?

Yes, the course is designed to be accessible to students regardless of their prior background in biology or mathematics. The curriculum is structured to introduce basic concepts progressively, allowing students to build their knowledge gradually and successfully.

6. What infrastructure and facilities are available for students joining this course?

Students in this course have access to high-end, state-of-the-art infrastructure designed to support advanced learning and research. This includes access to high-performance computing resources such as high-end PCs, immersive technologies like VR headsets, and specialized medical equipment including EEG, EMG, and fNIRS devices. A particularly noteworthy facility is the **virtual dissection table** equipped with a **virtual human cadaver.** All these resources are specifically provided to significantly enhance the hands-on learning experience and practical understanding for students.

7. Is there a greater emphasis on technology or biological sciences within the curriculum?

The course is structured to provide a well-balanced mix of both technology and biology. This is reflected in core subjects such as “Cell and Molecular Biology” alongside “AI in Healthcare,” ensuring students are comprehensively prepared for roles at the intersection of technology and healthcare.

8. Will I learn about both medical devices and biological sciences in this program?

The curriculum comprehensively covers both medical devices and biological sciences. Courses such as “Devices for Diagnostics and Therapeutics” provide insight into medical technology, while subjects like “Cell and Molecular Biology” and “Applied Human Anatomy and Physiology” cover fundamental biological principles.

9. How important are skills like programming and AI in this program compared to traditional Biotechnology?

Skills such as programming and AI are significant in this program, reflecting their critical and growing role in modern healthcare technology. The curriculum includes dedicated courses, such as those on AI in healthcare, highlighting their much more prominent role compared to traditional Biotechnology programs.

10. Is practical training or internship an integral part of the curriculum?

Yes, practical training through internships is a mandatory and integral component of the curriculum. Internships such as the “Clinical Science Internship” and “Industrial Internship” are required to ensure students gain essential hands-on experience and develop practical skills relevant to the field.

11. Are there more opportunities for practical projects and hands-on experiences in this course than in traditional biology programs?

Yes, the program strongly emphasizes practical experience and hands-on learning. This is facilitated through dedicated projects which encourage students to develop alpha and beta level prototypes for practical problems and mandatory “Industrial Internship,” which focuses on applying theoretical knowledge to real-world challenges.

12. What is the future scope of this course in the industry?

The future scope is highly promising due to the increasing integration of technology in healthcare globally. There is a significant and growing demand for professionals who possess expertise in both healthcare science and technology. This course opens up numerous opportunities in burgeoning fields such as med-tech, digital health, and AI/ML in healthcare.

13. In what ways will this course help me build a successful career?

By providing a strong foundation in both health science and technology, the course equips you with a versatile skill set that is highly valued across the industry. This preparation makes you ready for a wide array of roles in healthcare companies, technology firms, and research institutions, setting the stage for a successful and impactful career.

14. What are the opportunities for pursuing higher studies or careers abroad after this course?

Upon completion of the course, graduates have excellent opportunities for both higher education and careers internationally. You can pursue advanced studies in health science or health technology at universities abroad, or seek employment in international healthcare companies, research institutes, or health-tech firms.

15. What are the job opportunities available after completing this course?

The students will be well-prepared for diverse career paths in the healthcare technology sector. Potential roles exist in hospitals, health tech companies, and research organizations. Specific areas include healthcare data analysis and working with cutting-edge technologies like wearable health devices.

16. How effective is the placement support provided for students?

The program offers robust placement support designed to help students transition successfully into the workforce. This includes valuable internship opportunities, personalized career counselling, and strategic connections with leading healthcare companies to facilitate job placement after graduation.
