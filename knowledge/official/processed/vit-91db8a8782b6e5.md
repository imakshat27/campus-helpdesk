Which VIT UG programmes require VITEEE?

Only B.Tech programmes require VITEEE. All other undergraduate programmes including B.Des, B.Arch, B.Sc., BBA, B.Com., BCA, and B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture admit students through direct application.

Which VIT UG courses are available after Class 12 Science?

Class 12 Science students can apply for B.Tech (through VITEEE), B.Sc. Computer Science, BCA, B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, B.Sc. Multimedia and Animation, B.Sc. Visual Communication, B.Des, B.Arch, BBA, and B.Com, subject to the prescribed eligibility.

Which VIT UG courses are available after Class 12 Commerce?

Class 12 Commerce students can apply for B.Com, BBA, BBA (2+2), BCA, the UG Science and Humanities programmes, and B.Des, subject to the prescribed eligibility.

Can students apply to multiple VIT UG programmes?

Yes. Students can apply to multiple UG programmes through the respective official portals. Each programme has its own application form and fee.

Are scholarships available for VIT UG students?

Yes. VIT offers merit-based scholarships linked to entrance examination rank and Class 12 board performance. Government scholarships and Vidya Lakshmi loans are also accepted.

How do I choose the right VIT UG programme?

Match your Class 12 subject background and career direction to the programme. B.Tech suits engineering aspirants who clear VITEEE. B.Des and B.Arch suit students drawn to design and architecture. B.Sc., BCA, B.Com, BBA, and B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture suit students with specific subject interests.

B.Tech through VITEEE 2026 covers all four campuses. Other UG course availability varies by campus. Use the campus selector on vit.ac.in/all-courses/ug to view the list at each campus.
