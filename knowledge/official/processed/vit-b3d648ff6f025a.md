### How many programmes does VIT offer in 2027-28?

### Which VIT programmes require which entrance exam?

VITEEE 2027 is required for B.Tech. VITMEE 2026 is required for M.Tech, MCA, M.Des, and select M.Sc programmes. MBA admission accepts VITBEE, CAT, XAT, or MAT. Doctoral programmes use VITREE 2026. Non-engineering UG and several PG programmes admit students directly without an entrance test.

### Which VIT programmes admit students without an entrance test?

B.Des, B.Arch, B.Sc, B.Com, BBA, BCA, B.Sc (Hons.) Agriculture, MSW, M.Arch, LL.M, 5-Year Integrated M.Tech, Integrated M.Sc, and several M.Sc programmes (non-VITMEE streams) admit students directly through application, based on academic record.

### What is VITMEE 2026 and when is the deadline?

VITMEE 2026 is the VIT Master’s Entrance Examination, required for M.Tech, MCA, M.Des, and select M.Sc programmes (AI&ML, Data Science). The last date to apply is 12 April 2026.

### Can I apply for multiple VIT programmes?

Yes. Students can apply to multiple programmes through the respective official application portals. Each programme has its own application form, entrance route, and application fee.

### Are scholarships available for VIT students?

Yes. VIT offers merit-based scholarships linked to entrance examination performance and academic record, available for both UG and PG students. Government scholarships and Vidya Lakshmi education loans are also accepted.

### How do I apply for a Ph.D at VIT?

Apply through VITREE 2026, VIT’s research entrance examination. January and July sessions are available. Direct Ph.D and Ph.D (Deep Tech.) tracks are open to outstanding candidates. The Research Guide Directory is published before each session.

### What is the Integrated 5-Year programme at VIT?

Integrated 5-Year programmes combine bachelor’s and master’s-level study with seamless progression. VIT offers Integrated M.Tech (across multiple CSE and AI tracks), Integrated M.Sc (Biotechnology, Food Science, Data Science, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Applied Psychology), and Integrated Law (BA LL.B and BBA LL.B). Several Integrated M.Sc programmes include early-exit options for B.Sc or B.Sc Honours degrees.
