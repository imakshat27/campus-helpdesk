# Student services handbook

Sample information for this prototype. Not an official VIT publication. Scenario reference date: 16 October 2026.

## Student Support Office
Visit MB 012, Main Building ground floor, Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. The lunch break is 1:00 PM–2:00 PM.

## ID replacement
Report a lost ID through Student services → ID replacement. Upload a recent photo and pay the demo fee of ₹200. Bring the application receipt to collect the replacement after three working days.

## Certificates and attendance
Bonafide applications are under Student services → Certificates → Bonafide; normal processing is two working days. Report attendance discrepancies to course faculty within three working days with the course code, session date, and evidence.
