# Laboratory access guide

Sample information for this prototype. Not an official VIT publication. Scenario reference date: 16 October 2026.

## Booking and supervision
Request a workstation through Student portal → Academics → Lab booking at least one working day before the session. Include the lab name, preferred time, and faculty supervisor. A booking does not override scheduled teaching sessions.

## Robotics Lab
The Robotics Lab in TT 115 is open Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. Saturday project access is 9:00 AM–1:00 PM and requires prior faculty approval.

## Computer and Chemistry Labs
The Computer Lab in SJT 201 is open Monday–Saturday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM. The Chemistry Lab in MB 110 offers scheduled practicals Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–4:00 PM; no walk-in access. Bring your ID, lab coat, closed-toe shoes, and practical record for chemistry sessions.
