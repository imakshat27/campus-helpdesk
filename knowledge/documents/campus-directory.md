# Campus building directory

Sample information for this prototype. Not an official VIT publication. Scenario reference date: 16 October 2026.

## Silver Jubilee Tower (SJT)
SJT houses computing classrooms and the Computer Lab. SJT 301 is on the third floor, SJT 201 on the second floor, and the IT Helpdesk in SJT 005 on the ground floor.

## Technology Tower (TT)
TT houses engineering classrooms. TT 204 is on the second floor. The Robotics Lab is in TT 115 on the first floor.

## Main Building (MB)
MB 101 is on the first floor. The Chemistry Lab is in MB 110 on the first floor. The Student Support Office is in MB 012 on the ground floor.
