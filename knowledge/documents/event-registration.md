# October 2026 event guide

Sample information for this prototype. Not an official VIT publication. Scenario reference date: 16 October 2026.

## Campus Hackathon 2026
Register through Student portal → Events → Campus Hackathon 2026 by 20 October 2026, 6:00 PM IST. Teams have 2–4 students. The event is on 23 October 2026 in Anna Auditorium.

## Research Expo 2026
Register through Student portal → Events → Research Expo by 22 October 2026, 5:00 PM IST. Individual and team submissions are accepted. The event is on 28 October 2026 in the Convention Centre.

## Confirmation and changes
Find registration IDs in Events → My registrations. Edit team details before the registration deadline. After the deadline, contact the event coordinator listed on the event page.
